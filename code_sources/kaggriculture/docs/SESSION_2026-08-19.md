## 1. Two simulator bugs, both caught by cross-checking against ground truth

`planner/simulate.py` is a pure-Python port of the engine, ~1,200 steps/sec vs
kaggle_environments' ~140 — the thing that makes everything below affordable.

| bug | effect | how it surfaced |
|---|---|---|
| `run_episode` ran `episodeSteps` (720) interpreter calls instead of `episodeSteps-1` (719) | double-fired day 29's end-of-day refresh; cost ~600 in a real matchup | live self-play cross-check vs real engine |
| agents invoked with `obs` only | `kaggle_environments/agent.py:169-172` passes `[observation, configuration]` truncated to `co_argcount`; **5 of 9 pool agents** are `agent(obs, config=None)` and were running on fallback constants | signature audit after HTDC's `FARM_HAND_COST_MULT=10` fallback looked wrong |

**The replay-fidelity test could not have caught the second bug.** It replays
*recorded actions* and never invokes an agent. Added
`planner/tests/test_agent_fidelity.py` — live play, real agent code both sides —
which now shows **18/18 exact** final banks vs kaggle_environments.

Two independent fidelity guarantees now exist:
- `test_sim_fidelity.py` — 56 real replays, zero divergence on every field/step.
- `test_agent_fidelity.py` — 18/18 exact live games.

## 4. Top-of-ladder replay analysis — revises HANDOFF §0's diagnosis

`planner/analyze_top.py` recovers *realised* per-unit prices via a commit hook
(the replay's action list only carries requested quantities; an order for 100
units at an opening price of $200 realises far less down the curve).

23 replays, current top 8 + our own games:

| team | money | hires | hands | ops | units | $/unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ReCurSiON | 154,420 | 262 | 14.0 | 2826 | 1404 | **129.6** |
| Thomas Tschinkel | 150,794 | 277 | 12.0 | 2740 | 1532 | 123.3 |
| peikopon | 148,338 | 277 | 12.0 | 2763 | 1634 | 115.2 |
| **OURS (v3)** | **88,962** | **277** | **12.0** | **2858** | **1813** | **80.4** |

**Our labour already matches or exceeds every leader** — we run the *most* ops
of anyone. HANDOFF §0's "12 vs 14 hand slots, ~22% less labour" is not what
separates us; only ReCurSiON runs 14 hands. The entire gap is **price
realisation**: we sell the most units at the lowest price.

Caveat that mattered: leader replays are their *biggest wins*, i.e. weak
opponents and low market pressure, which inflates their prices. The implied
hypothesis (wrong dump item set) was therefore tested under controlled CRN
rather than read off the replays — and refuted. The confound was real.
